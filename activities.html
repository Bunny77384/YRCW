Activities & Events

Environment NSS Collab
June 05, 2024

Kumara Parvata Biodiversity Trek

A challenging expedition to the 2nd Highest Peak in Coorg (1,712m) within the Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary. Organized with NSS, this trek focused on the conservation of the unique Shola Forests and indigenous biodiversity.

Expedition Highlights:

  • Joint YRCW & NSS Initiative
  • Scaled 1,712m Peak via Bhattara Mane
  • Study of Shola Forests & Flora
  • Eco-Cleanup Drive on Trail

Annual Blood Donation Camp
